Case Summary

Rick Allen, a long-time resident of Delphi, Indiana, is serving a 130-year sentence for a crime the prosecution was unable to find any physical or digital evidence connecting him to. Despite growing concerns about investigative errors and prosecutorial overreach, the trial court withheld a substantial number of exhibits from the public, restricting access to a select group of journalists allotted 15 minutes per day.

Mr. Allen is now on direct appeal. The Appellant's Brief was filed Dec. 17, 2025; the Appellee Brief on Mar. 25, 2026; the Reply Brief on Apr. 27, 2026. Oral arguments were heard on Sept. 21, 2026 before a three-judge panel: Nancy H. Vaidik, Elaine B. Brown, and Robert R. Altice, Jr. See The Panel for judge profiles and The Appeal for issue-by-issue analysis.
